About Us

Welcome to Celini Retreat, our family-run vacation rental in the heart of Agios Prokopios, where hospitality is more than a tradition – it’s our passion. For years, our family has poured love and care into creating a place where guests feel at home, surrounded by the beauty of the Aegean. Whether you’re here for sun, sea, or simply to unwind, we’re here to make sure your stay is as warm and welcoming as the Greek sunshine.
